# Retro — harness adoption in flowspace3 (first contact)

## What happened

The CLI was already installed globally (v0.13.0) and the eng-harness skills were
already loaded in this session, so adoption started at the substrate: skills
installed project-local, `harness init`, then the two nucleus extensions.

## What was easy

- `harness init` + `harness new` are a two-command path from nothing to a loadable
  verb. `harness doctor --json` confirmed both extensions loaded without ambiguity.
- The envelope contract made "honest degraded" trivial to express — the awkward
  case (a gate with nothing to gate) has a first-class shape, so nothing had to
  be faked or hidden.

## What was hard

Only DL-001 above, and it was self-inflicted: the scaffold stub does not point at
`harness docs authoring-verbs`, so I went the long way round to the ctx API.

## Magic wand

*One line in the `harness new` scaffold naming `harness docs authoring-verbs`.*
Cheapest possible fix, and it lands on the exact moment an author needs it — the
blank `run()` they are staring at.

## What did I have to infer that the harness should have proved?

**That this is a Rust project.** Nothing executable says so. `.gitignore` is the
only evidence, and a gitignore is a claim, not a proof. The first `cargo`
invocation over a real `Cargo.toml` will settle it — until then both verbs carry
the assumption in their `next_action` rather than hiding it.
